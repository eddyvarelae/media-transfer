# Why our own signal handlers: Electron answers the first SIGTERM/SIGINT itself (a quit) and resets the signal to its default action; before this fix the second SIGTERM killed the process mid-finalization (npm wrapper: 'Electron exited with signal SIGTERM'). Installing process.on handlers at startup broke even the first signal, so they are installed in before-quit (holdSignals), after Electron's own handling.
