## FINDING 4b: notifications.json emptied by a SIGTERM during a transfer
The bell above has 6 entries; every entry older than 02:24:27 (VR-6's eight, and VR-7 check 1(a)'s TsDestX5 completion) had vanished.
Repro x2 (term-mid-transfer.sh, plain SIGTERM 1.5 s into a throttled transfer, no hook):
  run 1: notifications.json before = 6 items (4b-notifications-before-sigterm.json); exit 0.11 s; after = 0 bytes (4b-notifications-after-sigterm.json)
  run 2: relaunch on the 0-byte file -> still 0 bytes after launch (not repaired, not reported); SIGTERM again -> 0 bytes
  profiles.json, settings.json, temps.json, telemetry-queue.json: all parse after both runs.
Cause (code reading): electron/notifications.ts:67 writes with fsp.writeFile (truncate, then write); the quit finalization records the cancelled transfer
(main.ts recordTransfer -> addTransferNotification) and the process exits before that write completes. With MT_TEST_FINALIZE_DELAY_MS=1500 (check 1c) the write finished and the cancelled entry exists.
Effect on a real install: a quit during a transfer wipes the bell history AND the persisted new-drive dismissals (same file); the next write starts a fresh list.
